Parent Menu

Adult controls are hidden to reduce accidental taps. Press and hold the top-right corner for about 2 seconds to open this menu.

Communication tip: When she taps a button, repeat it naturally. Example: she taps drink, adult says, "Drink. Want drink."
Routine tip: Use the same short words for transitions. Example: "First get dressed, then car."
First / Then tip: Tap First or Then, then pick a picture. Use Repeat if the full sentence needs to be modeled again.
